📖 How to Play Ludo

🎯 Goal of the Game

Move all 4 of your colored tokens out of your base, around the perimeter track, and into the center home triangle before any opponent.

🎲 Rolling a 6

  • A roll of 6 is required to bring a token out of the yard onto the starting square.
  • Rolling a 6 grants an EXTRA ROLL!
  • Rolling 3 consecutive sixes forfeits the turn to prevent infinite rolls.

⚔️ Capturing & Safe Squares

  • Landing on an opponent's token on any regular track square captures it! The captured token is sent back to its base, and you get an EXTRA ROLL!
  • Tokens on Safe Squares (marked with a Star ★ and shield) CANNOT be captured. Multiple players' tokens can safely coexist there.

🏁 Finishing

  • Tokens enter their player-colored home corridor and require an exact roll to reach the final center finish.
  • Finishing a token awards a bonus extra roll! First player to finish all 4 tokens wins! 🏆
