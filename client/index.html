📖 Standard Ludo Rules

🎯 Goal

Guide all 4 of your colored tokens from your Home Yard around the 52-square track and into the Center Finishing Triangle before any opponent.

🎲 Rolling a 6 & Extra Turns

  • A roll of 6 is required to release a token from the yard onto the starting square.
  • Rolling a 6 grants an EXTRA ROLL!
  • Three 6s rule: Rolling three consecutive 6s forfeits the turn to prevent infinite rolling.

⚔️ Capturing & Safe Squares

  • Landing on an opponent's token on any regular track square captures it! The opponent token returns to its yard, and you get an EXTRA ROLL!
  • Tokens resting on Safe Squares (marked with a Star ★) CANNOT be captured. Opponent tokens can safely share safe squares.

🏁 Home Path & Exact Finish

  • After completing a full circuit, tokens enter their player-colored home runway.
  • Tokens require an exact dice roll to enter the final center triangle.
  • Finishing a token awards a bonus extra roll!

🏆 Winning

The first player to get all 4 tokens into the center finish wins the game!
